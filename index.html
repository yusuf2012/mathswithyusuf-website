About

The Thinking Behind This

Hi, I'm Yusuf — a mathematics educator with a Master's degree in Mathematics from the University of Cape Town (UCT) and over 15 years of teaching experience across secondary school, university, and adult education.

Too many people believe Maths is something that only happens to certain people. It isn't. The subject is completely learnable — at any age, at any level — if it's taught clearly, step by step, without rushing past the parts that actually matter.

Whether you're a Grade 8 student building your foundation, a university student tackling calculus or linear algebra, or simply someone who wants to finally understand the maths they never quite got — these lessons are for you.

Every lesson follows the same structure: concept first, then a worked example, then a practice question you can try yourself. No jargon. No skipping steps. No paywall.

Clarity First
Every concept is unpacked from scratch. We don't assume you already know — we explain it properly, then show you how it works.
Every Level, Every Topic
From Grade 8 algebra to university-level calculus, linear algebra, and beyond — plus short lessons for everyday maths that anyone can benefit from.
Free. Always.
All YouTube lessons are completely free. A paid study pack is available for extra practice — but the core teaching never sits behind a paywall.
